# Release 1

10 October 2026. Source with a build script, for the Gwent public beta
client 0.9.24.3.432.

## What it is

A Monte Carlo Tree Search player for an imperfect-information game. Before
each move it plays thousands of imagined games with the game's own rules
engine. Your hidden cards are filled in by a belief model: a deck posterior
over the known 2018 decks, given the cards seen so far, and an opponent
model fitted on recorded games. A policy network suggests which moves to try
first; a small logistic model, fitted on games, scores each position at the
end of the current round as a chance to win the match. It sees only what a human in its seat would see.

## Check your build

The build reads the card tables from your client and prints:

```text
card table: sha256 db0dbb0424c3e29b
```

The same value means your client matches the one this release was measured
with. When the game starts, the loader's log (`MelonLoader/Latest.log` in
the client folder) shows the AI's start line, ending with:

```text
policy prior pol2 sha256 b4acc8471aa94298
```

That is the policy network after the AI filled in its card features from
your client; the same value means it is the network that was measured.

## Known limits

- It looks ahead within the current round; later rounds come from the
  evaluator's score.
- It thinks about 10 seconds on a normal move and up to 30 on a hard one,
  on helper processes (`min(6, cores - 2)`). Fewer cores make it weaker.
- Tested on Linux (Fedora 44). macOS on Apple silicon is documented. Not
  tested on Windows yet.
